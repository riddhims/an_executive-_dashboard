# 🇩🇪 Germany Bakery Sales Intelligence

### Turning Bakery Sales Data into Actionable Business Insights

## 📊 Project Overview

Germany Bakery Sales Intelligence is an end-to-end Business Intelligence project developed to analyse bakery sales performance across different regions, products, categories and sales channels in Germany.

The project demonstrates how raw sales data can be transformed into meaningful business insights through KPI development, data analysis and interactive Tableau visualisations.

The goal is to provide a clear overview of business performance, identify sales trends, understand product profitability and support data-driven decision-making.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Monitor overall sales and revenue performance.
- Identify top-performing bakery products and categories.
- Analyse regional sales performance across Germany.
- Evaluate profitability and gross margin.
- Compare sales performance across distribution channels.
- Understand monthly revenue trends and growth patterns.

## 📈 Key Performance Indicators

| KPI | Business Purpose |
|---|---|
| Total Revenue | Measure overall sales performance |
| Total Units Sold | Understand sales volume |
| Gross Margin | Evaluate profitability after product costs |
| Gross Margin % | Measure margin performance |
| Average Selling Price | Analyse average revenue per unit |
| Monthly Revenue Growth | Track revenue development over time |

## 📊 Dashboard Features

- Executive sales performance overview
- Product and category performance analysis
- Regional sales analysis
- Sales channel comparison
- Monthly revenue trend analysis
- Interactive filters for date, region, category and sales channel

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Tableau Desktop:** Interactive dashboards and data visualisation
- **Microsoft Excel / CSV:** Dataset storage and data exploration
- **Calculated Fields:** KPI development and business calculations
- **Business Intelligence:** Performance monitoring and analytical insights

## 📂 Dataset Overview

The project uses a synthetic bakery sales dataset representing sales activity across Germany.

Key dimensions include:
- Date
- Product and category
- Region
- Sales channel

Key measures include:
- Units sold
- Unit price
- Revenue
- Product cost
- Gross margin
- Discount percentage

**Note:** The dataset is entirely synthetic and created for educational and portfolio purposes. It does not represent actual company sales, market statistics or confidential business information.

## 💡 Business Value

This project demonstrates how Business Intelligence can help organisations move beyond traditional reporting by transforming sales data into actionable insights.

It highlights the importance of KPI-driven performance monitoring, regional analysis, product profitability and interactive data exploration.

## 🚀 Project Outcome

An interactive Tableau dashboard that brings together key sales metrics and business perspectives in one analytical environment.
